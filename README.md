## ⚡ What's Included
- **Instant Flow**: Open site ➔ Click **START RUN** ➔ Seamless infinite procedural gauntlet.
- **Procedural Kinematics & IK**: 2-bone analytic IK solver with Verlet blonde ponytail ribbon.
- **Full Combat**: 6-hit combo progression, jump web zips, slide sweeps, and Perfect Parries.
- **Micro Audio Synthesizer**: Pure procedural Web Audio sound generation for hits, kicks, jumps, and parries.
- **High Score & Distance Tracker**: Best distance & score records saved locally.

## 🕹️ Controls

### Keyboard:
- **`A` / `D`**: Run Left / Right
- **`W` / `Up`**: Jump & Wall-Kick
- **`Spacebar` / `E`**: 6-Hit Martial Arts Attack Combo
- **`S` (Hold)**: Perfect Parry & Stun Counters
- **`S` (During sprint)**: Low Slide Sweep
- **`Up` (In Air)**: Web Zip Lift

### Touch / Mobile:
- **Left Screen Area**: Virtual thumb joystick
- **Right Screen Area**: Tap to attack, swipe to slide / web-zip
